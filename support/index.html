Captain Patch: Cat Puzzle – Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to nomad70989@gmail.com. Please mention your device and the island number if something went wrong.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag Captain Patch over every sand tile exactly once, eat the fish on the step their number shows, and end in the hammock. The first islands explain each new rule with a picture card, and “How to play” on the title screen shows all of them.

I got a new iPhone or iPad. Is my progress gone? If you are signed in to iCloud, the game restores your progress on the new device by itself.

How do I switch to the kids’ islands, change the language or the colours? Tap the gear on the title screen or the map to open Settings.

Game Center achievements and the Daily Island leaderboard are optional; sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings to use them.
